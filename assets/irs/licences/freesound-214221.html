World's 'longest-echo' 5th impulse

Overall rating (13 ratings)
acs272

January 17th, 2014

Follow
Soundscapes > Synthetic / Artificial
Inchindown

Measured for Sonic Wonderland/The Sound Book, this is an uncompressed impulse response from the space which holds the Guinness World Record for the 'longest echo'. It is a WWII oil storage tank in Scotland. Impulse response measured using 1/4" measurement microphone and a starting pistol.
