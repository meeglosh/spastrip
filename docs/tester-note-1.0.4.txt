SPAStrip 1.0.4

My dudes,

This one brings SPAStrip onto the same effects as SPASynth 1.0.33: steadier reverb level, long tails that ring out, and a redesigned LIMIT panel.

CHANGES THAT ARE ON PURPOSE (please don't report these as bugs)
- Reverb at a short DECAY or a large SIZE is now audible. It used to
  nearly vanish there, and small SIZE used to jump out. Level is now
  steady across the range. Existing sessions with reverb will sound a
  little different.
- GLITTER's FREEZE switch is gone. RELEASE at its maximum holds the cloud.
  Old sessions with FREEZE on open with RELEASE at max.
- The LIMIT panel shows THRESHOLD instead of DRIVE. Existing sessions
  sound the same.

WHAT'S NEW
- Reverb level is steady across SIZE and DECAY in every mode.
- Long delay and GLITTER tails are no longer cut off by the host. This
  fixes Phil's report of delay tails stopping early.
- GLITTER RELEASE now lasts as long as the knob says (30 s fades in about
  30 s). With PITCH or SPREAD PITCH it still leaves the audible range
  sooner, by nature.
- EQ analyzer PRE / POST now show the signal entering and leaving the EQ,
  wherever it sits in the chain.
- LIMIT panel redesign: THRESHOLD and CEILING faders between the input
  and output meters, with a link icon. Captions read LOOK AHEAD and
  STEREO LINK.
- Tooltips wait a moment before appearing when you move between controls.

INSTALL
1. Quit your DAW first (the Mac installer will ask you to if Logic,
   MainStage or GarageBand is open).
2. Run the installer (Mac .pkg, Windows .exe). Windows is still unsigned:
   click "More info" > "Run anyway".
3. Logic: if SPAStrip doesn't show up, Plug-in Manager > Reset & Rescan
   Selection, then restart Logic.

PLEASE TEST (A/B against 1.0.3 where you can)
4. Reverb: in every mode, sweep SIZE 0 to 100 and DECAY 0.25 s to 8 s.
   Does the level feel even? Anything that vanishes, jumps out or sounds
   too quiet or too loud?
5. Delay: set a long, high-feedback tail, play a note and bounce or stop
   the transport. Does the tail ring out in full?
6. GLITTER: RELEASE at 30 s, then at max. Does 30 s last about 30 s? Does
   max hold until you move it? Open an old session that used FREEZE.
7. LIMIT panel: use the THRESHOLD / CEILING faders and the link icon.
   Anything unclear, misdrawn or clipped?
8. EQ analyzer: move the EQ in the chain and compare PRE and POST.
9. Tooltips: move between controls. Do they wait a beat before showing?

Anything odd, send me the preset and what you were doing.
