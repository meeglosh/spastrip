SPAStrip Quickstart
===================
Version @VERSION@

1. INSERT IT
   Insert SPAStrip on a track or bus as an AU or VST3 effect. In Logic Pro,
   for example, click an Audio FX slot on the channel strip and choose
   Audio Units > Silverplatter Audio > SPAStrip. It starts with every effect
   off, so it is transparent until you turn something on. Each effect has an
   on/off switch in its tab.

2. THE CHAIN
   The tabs along the top of the window are the effects, in the order the
   audio flows through them (left to right): FILTER, DIST, CHORUS, MOD,
   TREM/VIB, GRAIN, DELAY, REVERB, CONV, EQ, COMP, LIMIT. Drag a tab to
   a new position to reorder the chain; a drag is a single undo step.

3. FILTERS
   The FILTER tab is a multimode filter with low-pass, high-pass, band-pass
   and notch modes at 12 and 24 dB per octave. The EQ tab is an eight-band
   parametric EQ. Both can sit anywhere in the chain.

4. RANDOMIZE ALL, WILD AND THE PADLOCKS
   RANDOMIZE ALL rolls a new setting for every effect, in one undo step. The
   WILD knob next to it sets how tame or unhinged the rolls are (double-click
   it to return to the middle). The padlock on a tab keeps that effect as it
   is through every roll: lock the ones you like, then roll the rest.

5. SIDECHAIN AND MOD SLOTS
   The modulation section follows a sidechain envelope: choose its SOURCE
   (Input, or External for your DAW's sidechain; see README.txt for the
   routing), set GAIN, ATTACK, RELEASE and HPF, and watch the ENVELOPE.
   There are eight mod slots. Each slot has a picker for the parameter it
   modulates and a DEPTH (positive or negative). The quick way: right-click
   any knob that can be modulated and choose "Assign to mod slot N"; right-click
   again to remove it.

6. FACTORY IMPULSE RESPONSES
   On the CONV tab, "Factory IR" lists nineteen built-in impulse responses
   (springs, plates, rooms, halls, churches, tanks, tunnels, a forest). You
   can also load your own WAV, AIFF or FLAC file, or drop one onto the panel.
   Their credits are in CREDITS.txt and under the SPAStrip menu > About.
   Long impulses at 4x oversampling are heavy on the CPU; see README.txt.

7. PRESETS, UNDO AND COLOUR
   Click the preset name to browse; SAVE stores your own, with an effect
   type (Drums, Bass, Vocals, Guitar, Keys, Synth, FX, Mixbus, Mastering,
   Creative) so you can filter by type later. INIT resets everything. The
   undo and redo arrows (or Cmd/Ctrl+Z) step back through your changes,
   including rolls and reordering. The round colour button in the header
   changes SPAStrip's accent colour.

Have fun. RANDOMIZE ALL is the tour guide.
